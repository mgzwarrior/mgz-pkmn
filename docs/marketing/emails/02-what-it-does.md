A quick tour.

Drop a want-list into the editor — natural language is fine:

```
top 5 Mew cards under $50
all Charizard cards | japanese
rest of Surging Sparks
```

Hit run, and you get back:

- **A printable PDF binder** — thumbnail, set, number, and your price floor for every card. Fold it up, slip it in a backpack pocket, walk the booths.
- **A set checklist** — what you own, what you still need, by set, on one page. Useful before you even leave the house.
- **A spreadsheet** — same data, but sortable. Comp tiers included.

Prices are cross-checked across three open sources so nothing's coming from one suspicious vendor.
