Last one for now — where it's going.

mgz-pkmn started as a show-prep tool, but the heart of it is collectors, and that's the direction I'm building toward. The next big stretch (v1.9) is a workspace rethink: your collections, wishlists, and saved searches as first-class things that feel good on your phone and your desktop, not bolted on.

Before that, the current release line is a plain honesty pass — every screen that explains the app brought back in sync with what it actually does, so nothing oversells. (The committed roadmap lives in docs/roadmap.md.)

Here's where you come in. I want to hear from collectors:

  • What do you track today, and where does it fall apart?
  • What would make you reach for this over a notes app or a spreadsheet?

"This is confusing" is useful. "I wish it did X" is useful. The half-formed thought is the one I most want to hear — drop it in Discussions or just reply to this email.

That's it from the welcome side. From here, I'll only write when a real version ships. Thanks for being early.
