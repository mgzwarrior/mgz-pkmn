Hey — thanks for signing up.

You collect because it's fun: the art, the hunt, the box you keep meaning to sort. The part that's not fun is the spreadsheet — tracking what you own, what you're chasing, and what any of it is worth today without twelve browser tabs open.

That's what I built mgz-pkmn for. It's a Pokémon TCG tool that helps you see your collection clearly: flip through cards and save the ones you like, walk a whole set, price anything against the live market, and keep your "own" and "chasing" lists in one place.

The easiest way in is Swipe — it shows you one card at a time, you swipe to pass, keep, or love, and it learns what you're into. Tap any card for the full-size art and its current price.
