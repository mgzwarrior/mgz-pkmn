Hey — thanks for signing up.

You're here for the code, so I'll keep the pitch short. **mgz-pkmn** is a Pokémon TCG collection-and-pricing tool, MIT-licensed and built entirely in the open. The CHANGELOG, the roadmap, the half-formed ideas in Discussions — all on GitHub.

The shape of it:

- A **Python** core + CLI (the lookup, parsing, and pricing engine).
- A **FastAPI** service that streams results over Server-Sent Events and backs the hosted demo.
- A **React + TypeScript** SPA (Vite, Tailwind v4, a token-driven design system).
- An **Astro** marketing site — the one this email came from.
