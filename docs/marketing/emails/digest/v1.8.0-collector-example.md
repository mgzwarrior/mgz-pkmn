Quick one — something real shipped.

**Swipe is now where the app opens.** One card at a time, swipe to pass, keep, or love — it's the gentlest way into a collection, so it's the first thing you see now instead of a search bar. The discovery bar reads Swipe → Browse → Search, in that order, and Swipe picked up its own card-deck icon so it's easy to spot.

💡 First time back in a while? The Help modal now walks a real sample card and points at Swipe first — worth a look even if you've been here before.

[**Open Swipe →**](https://mgz-pkmn.onrender.com)

Also in v1.8.0: the guided Tour now drives the app through every surface instead of just narrating it, and this welcome sequence itself moved onto a proper reason-branched drip — the one you're reading.
