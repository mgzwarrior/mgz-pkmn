Last one for now — where the prep side is headed.

The current release line is an honesty pass: every screen that explains the app brought back in sync with what it actually does, so nothing oversells before a show. After that, the export side keeps getting richer — more binder layouts, an HTML view you can pull up on your phone, and a swipeable flipbook for quick review at the table. (Committed roadmap: [docs/roadmap.md](https://github.com/mgzwarrior/mgz-pkmn/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md).)

But the best roadmap input comes from people who actually work shows. So:

- What does your prep look like now, and where does it cost you time or money?
- What would you want printed and in your hand at the booth?

A real wishlist that came back wrong is the most useful thing you can send me — reply with it, or open an issue. "Would you ship X?" is a perfectly good [issue](https://github.com/mgzwarrior/mgz-pkmn/issues).

That's it from the welcome side — from here I'll only write when a real version ships. Good luck at the next show.
