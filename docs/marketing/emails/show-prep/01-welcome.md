You know the moment: you're three booths deep, holding the wishlist you scribbled in the parking lot. Someone behind you sighs. The Wi-Fi crawls. You look up a comp on your phone and the page hangs while the dealer waits.

That's the moment I built **mgz-pkmn** for.
